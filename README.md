# Ramadhan Tracker 🌙

A comprehensive companion app to track your daily worship and spiritual progress during the Holy Month of Ramadhan.

---

## 📱 Google Play Store & APKPure Description

### 🇬🇧 English

**Short Description (80 chars):**
Track your Ramadhan journey with prayer times, daily tasks, and gamified progress.

**Full Description:**

**Ramadhan Tracker** is your ultimate companion for a productive and spiritual Holy Month. Stay consistent with your daily _Ibadah_ using our comprehensive tracking features designed to keep you motivated.

**Key Features:**

- **📋 Daily Activity Tracker**: Monitor your Salah, Quran reading, Charity, and Sunnah prayers effortlessly.
- **🎮 Gamification System**: Level up your spiritual journey! Earn XP for every activity and unlock exclusive **Achievements** like "7 Day Streak" or "Khatam Reader".
- **⏰ Prayer Times & Notifications**: Accurate prayer times based on your location. Get reminders for Sahur, Iftar, and all 5 daily prayers.
- **📊 Statistics & Insights**: Visualize your consistency with beautiful interactive charts and heatmaps.
- **widgets Home Screen Widget**: View your next prayer time and daily progress percentage at a glance without opening the app.
- **🌍 Multi-language Support**: Fully localized in English and **Bahasa Indonesia**.
- **🌙 Dark Mode**: Comfortable viewing for nightly Ibadah tracking.

Download **Ramadhan Tracker** now and make this Ramadhan your best one yet!

**Ramadhan Tracker** adalah teman setia untuk bulan suci yang lebih produktif dan berkah. Jaga konsistensi ibadahmu dengan fitur pelacakan yang lengkap, menyenangkan, dan memotivasi.

- **⏰ Jadwal Sholat & Notifikasi**: Jadwal akurat sesuai lokasimu. Dapatkan pengingat waktu Sahur, Buka Puasa, dan Adzan setiap waktu sholat.

Unduh **Ramadhan Tracker** sekarang dan jadikan Ramadhan tahun ini lebih baik dari sebelumnya!
